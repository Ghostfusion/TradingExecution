# Alpaca platform state, 2026-09-12 (execution-layer reference)

**Bottom line**
1. The pre-2026 PDT regime is dead: FINRA Rule 4210 intraday-margin standards (Notice 26-10) are effective 2026-06-04 with a phase-in to 2027-10-20 [20]; Alpaca deleted `daytrade_count`, `daytrading_buying_power` and `pattern_day_trader` from `GET /v2/account` on 2026-07-06 [18], and the binding constraint is now real-time IML/IMD, not a trade count [3][27].
2. The order surface still looks like 2025 (5 order types, 4 order classes) but the constraint lattice is where systems die: extended-hours and overnight accept **only** `limit` + `day|gtc` [1][12], brackets forbid `extended_hours` [1], fractional/notional is `DAY`-only, cannot be shorted, and cannot carry advanced order classes [4][17], `notional` orders cannot be replaced at all [1], and price precision is 2 decimals ≥ $1.00 / 4 decimals < $1.00 or you get 42210000 [1].
3. Everything measured in paper is optimistic by the size of the real cost stack plus fill optimistics: paper fills at NBBO, ignores displayed size, randomises partial fills ~10% of the time, and models no impact, queue, slippage, dividends or regulatory fees [2] — while live costs are SEC $0.0000206 x trade value (sells), TAF $0.000195/share capped $9.79 (sells), CAT $0.000003/share (both sides) [21].
4. Buy the data tier you actually need: Basic = IEX only, 30 stream symbols, 200 REST calls/min, SIP blocked for the last 15 minutes; Algo Trader Plus = $99/mo, all exchanges (SIP), unlimited symbols, 10,000 REST calls/min, 1 concurrent stream connection per endpoint [8][9][14][16].

## Findings

### 1. Trading API order types, classes and legal combinations

1. **Order types (PROVEN).** Equities: `market`, `limit`, `stop`, `stop_limit`, `trailing_stop` [1]. `trailing_stop` requires exactly one of `trail_price` (dollar distance from high-water mark) or `trail_percent` (percent), exposes `hwm` and a derived, continuously-updated `stop_price`, does **not** trigger outside regular hours, accepts only `day`/`gtc`, and is not yet allowed as a bracket/OCO leg [1].
2. **Order classes (PROVEN).** `order_class` ∈ {`simple`, `bracket`, `oco`, `oto`} for equities; `mleg` exists but is options multi-leg [1][13]. Bracket = OTOCO of entry + `take_profit` + `stop_loss`; OCO = two same-side **exit** orders only (`type` must be `limit`; in `nested=true` responses the take-profit leg is the parent and the stop-loss the child); OTO = entry + exactly one of `take_profit`/`stop_loss` [1].
3. **Bracket/OCO/OTO rules (PROVEN).** `take_profit.limit_price` and `stop_loss.stop_price` are both mandatory; `stop_loss.limit_price` optional and promotes the leg to stop-limit; `take_profit.limit_price` must be above `stop_loss.stop_price` for a buy bracket (reverse for sell); `time_in_force` must be `day` or `gtc`; `extended_hours` must be false or omitted; every leg carries DNR/DNC so prices are not adjusted for dividends/corporate actions; a partial take-profit fill reduces the stop-loss quantity to the remainder [1].
4. **Stop-loss leg distance rule (PROVEN).** The stop price must be at least $0.01 below (sell) / above (buy) the "base price" — the take-profit limit price for OCO, the entry limit price for a limit-entry bracket/OTO, or the current market price otherwise; this exists to avoid race conditions [1].
5. **Trailing stop vs wash-trade guard (PROVEN).** Alpaca's wash-trade prevention rejects the *new* order with HTTP 403 whenever two of the account's own orders could interact (e.g. existing `limit buy` + new `limit sell` at or below the buy limit); bracket, OCO and trailing-stop orders are explicit exceptions, which is the sanctioned way to run simultaneous entry/exit on one symbol [11]. This is the real reason a naive "always keep a stop in" intraday sleeve gets 403s, and it applies in paper too [11].
6. **Replace/cancel semantics (PROVEN).** `PATCH /v2/orders/{id}` (a) returns success **without guaranteeing** replacement (the new order is rejected if the old one filled first, events arrive on `trade_updates`), (b) reduces buying power to the larger of old/new order during the replace, (c) is rejected while status ∈ {`accepted`, `pending_new`, `pending_cancel`, `pending_replace`}, and (d) **cannot** touch non-IPO notional orders — cancel and resubmit instead. `DELETE /v2/orders/{id}` returns 204 on acceptance and **422** if the order is no longer cancelable; `DELETE /v2/orders` returns 500 for any order that is no longer cancelable [13][26].
7. **Notional/fractional rules (PROVEN).** Fractional (`qty` with decimals) and notional (`notional`) orders take up to **9 decimal places**, require `time_in_force=day`, are accepted for `market`, `limit`, `stop`, `stop_limit` only, work in pre/post/overnight sessions, and require `fractionable=true` on the asset (otherwise `asset ... is not fractionable`, 403/40310000) [4][17]. `notional` works only with `market|limit|stop|stop_limit` + `day` and the minimum is $1.00 (`notional must be >= 1.00`) [17]. **Fractional orders cannot be sold short** (`fractional orders cannot be sold short`, 42210000) [4][17]. `qty` and `notional` are mutually exclusive; supplying both is rejected [4].
8. **`client_order_id` (PROVEN that duplicates are rejected; UNVERIFIED whether the window is forever).** Omitting it makes Alpaca generate one; it is returned on the order object and `GET /v2/orders:by_client_order_id` retrieves by it [1][13]. A duplicate raises 422 `client_order_id must be unique`, described by Alpaca as "a duplicate `client_order_id` ... for another active order" [17]. Whether a string may be reused after the original order reaches a terminal state is **not documented**; community guidance says every order needs a unique id [17]. Treat it as globally unique, not as an idempotency key.
9. **Precision (PROVEN).** Limit and stop prices: max **2 decimals** when the price ≥ $1.00, max **4 decimals** when < $1.00; violations return `{"code":42210000,"message":"invalid limit_price ... sub-penny increment does not fulfill minimum pricing criteria"}` [1].
10. **GTC ageing (PROVEN).** All GTC orders are auto-cancelled 90 days after creation; `expires_at` is exposed on list/get and the cancel job fires at **16:15 ET** on the expiry date; orders sit in `pending_cancel` until the venue confirms [1].
11. **`legs` and `nested` (PROVEN).** Bracket/OCO members are independent orders in `GET /v2/orders`; with `nested=true` the parent carries an array `legs`; nested legs of legs are `null` (OpenAPI 3.1 note, 2026-08-05) [1][13].

### 2. Time-in-force and session rules

12. **Enum and semantics (PROVEN).** `day` (RTH only unless `extended_hours=true`; cancelled after the close; queued to next session if submitted after close), `gtc` (not DNR-capable; 90-day ageing), `opg` (MOO/LOO in the opening auction only; **rejected if submitted after 09:28 ET and before 19:00 ET**, queued to the next day's open after 19:00), `cls` (MOC/LOC in the closing auction only; **rejected after 15:50 ET and before 19:00 ET**, queued after 19:00), `ioc`, `fok` [1].
13. **Type × TIF legality (PROVEN).** Whole-share USD equities: `gtc`/`day` allowed on all four basic types; `ioc`, `fok`, `opg`, `cls` allowed **only** on `market` and `limit` (stop and stop-limit are explicitly No) [1]. Fractional orders: `day` only (every other TIF is No) [1]. OTC assets: `gtc`/`day` on all four types, nothing else [1]. Trailing stop: `day`/`gtc` only [1].
14. **Extended hours & 24/5 (PROVEN).** Full extended hours are overnight 20:00-04:00 ET Sun-Fri, pre-market 04:00-09:30, after-hours 16:00-20:00 [1]. `extended_hours=true` requires a `limit` order with `day` or `gtc` — anything else is rejected with `extended hours order must be DAY or GTC limit orders` (42210000) [1][17]. Overnight (BOATS) supports **only** `limit` with `day|gtc`; `day` orders placed overnight live through the next day's pre/RTH/after-hours and die at 20:00 ET; overnight trades are dated to the *next* trade date (T+1 from there) [12]. Assets are flagged `overnight_tradable` / `overnight_halted`; sync the overnight universe between 19:45 and 20:00 ET (19:55 ideal; refresh loop starts 19:05, every 10 min) [12]. Orders not eligible for extended hours submitted after 16:00 queue for the next trading day [1].
15. **Regulatory-mandated type restriction (PROVEN).** IPO symbols only accept `limit` orders before the first trade; the asset model exposes an `ipo` attribute [1].

### 3. Paper vs live (what the comparison harness must correct for)

16. **Simulation model (PROVEN).** Paper matches against the best available current market price (NBBO) for every order, paper-only or not; fills happen only when the order becomes marketable (buy limit ≥ best ask, sell limit ≤ best bid); **order quantity is not checked against NBBO size**, so a giant order fills like a tiny one; when an order is eligible to fill it receives **partial fills for a random size 10% of the time**, and the remainder is re-evaluated if still marketable [2].
17. **Omissions (PROVEN).** Paper does not model market impact, information leakage, latency slippage, queue position for non-marketable limits, price improvement, **regulatory fees**, or **dividends**; borrow fees are "coming soon" [2]. Paper does not send fill emails and cannot change balance except by reset [2].
18. **Separate accounts/keys/environments (PROVEN).** Paper and live use different API keys and base URLs (`https://paper-api.alpaca.markets` vs `https://api.alpaca.markets`); the API spec is identical; new paper accounts default to **$100k** and are created/deleted (not reset) from the dashboard, requiring fresh keys [2]. Deleted/recreated keys are the top cause of 401/403 confusion — mismatched key↔environment returns 401 (sometimes 403) [17].
19. **Paper-only accounts are IEX-only (PROVEN).** A Paper Only account (global signup) "is only entitled to receive and make use of IEX market data" [2].

### 4. Buying power, margin and the post-PDT regime

20. **Multipliers (PROVEN).** `multiplier` ∈ {1, 2, 4}: 1 = cash/limited margin (buying_power = cash); 2 = Reg T margin (2x intraday **and** overnight); 4 = 4x intraday with 2x Reg T overnight [10]. `buying_power` formula is published per case: multiplier 4 → `(last_equity − last_maintenance_margin) × 4`; multiplier 2 → `max(equity − initial_margin, 0) × 2`; multiplier 1 → cash [10]. Alpaca's own headline remains "Up to 4X intraday & 2X overnight buying power" [13-ref: about-trading-api]. Margin/shorting requires **$2,000+ equity**; below that the account is restricted to **1x** [5][31].
21. **PDT retirement (PROVEN).** `pattern_day_trader`, `daytrade_count`, `daytrading_buying_power`, `dtbp_check`, `pdt_check`, `pd_*` endpoints all deprecated 2026-06-03 and **removed from account/config responses on 2026-07-06**; migration required because clients that hard-require those keys now fail validation [18]. Post-removal the account object has **no** day-trade counter and **no** `daytrading_buying_power` field [10][18].
22. **Field inventory on `GET /v2/account` (PROVEN unless flagged).** Present: `buying_power`, `regt_buying_power`, `non_marginable_buying_power`, `options_buying_power`, `multiplier`, `sma`, `cash`, `equity`, `last_equity`, `initial_margin`, `maintenance_margin`, `last_maintenance_margin`, `long_market_value`, `short_market_value`, `intraday_adjustments`, `accrued_fees`, `pending_reg_taf_fees`, `pending_transfer_in/out`, `shorting_enabled`, `trading_blocked`, `account_blocked`, `transfers_blocked`, `trade_suspended_by_user`, `status`. **UNVERIFIED:** `effective_buying_power` and `position_market_value` appear in Alpaca's documented 200-response *example* but are absent from the schema `properties`, so their semantics are undocumented [10][13].
23. **Buying-power check basis (PROVEN).** Opening long buys and short sells both consume buying power; open buy-long and sell-short orders keep consuming it until executed or cancelled; sell-long/buy-to-cover do not replenish until executed. Initial checks use: far side of the NBBO when the core session is open; inside-market **midpoint** during extended hours; the latest trade from market cache when both sessions are closed [1].
24. **Short-sale buying power (PROVEN).** Computed order value = `MAX(limit_price, 1.03 × current ask) × qty`; for market short orders it is simply `1.03 × ask × qty` [1].
25. **Margin mechanics (PROVEN).** Initial margin 50% marginable / 100% non-marginable; overnight maintenance: long <$2.50 → 100%, $2.50-$6.00 → 50%, >$6.00 → 30%, 2x LETF 50%, 3x LETF 75%; short <$5 → greater of $2.50/share or 100%, ≥$5 → greater of $5.00/share or 30%; single-name concentration ≥70% of equity MV with ≥$100k margin balance → 50% maintenance [5]. Margin interest 6.25% (non-Elite) / 4.75% (Elite 100k), charged only on the settlement-date debit balance, `× rate / 360`, so Friday settles carry 3 days [5][21].
26. **IML/IMD, the new hard gate (PROVEN).** IML = running maintenance-margin excess; an IML-reducing transaction (purchases, option shorts, long-option expiry, withdrawals) creates an **IMD** = the largest negative IML of the day; mark-to-market losses alone do **not** create an IMD. Alpaca says an IMD triggers a margin call to be satisfied within **two business days**; FINRA's capital-charge clock starts on the 6th business day and the deficit expires for capital purposes after 15 business days; a customer with a practice of unmet IMDs gets a 90-day freeze on new debits and new short positions. **De minimis waiver: no call if the unmet deficit is under $1,000 or 5% of equity, whichever is lower.** Intraday P&L counts toward buying power; FDIC bank-sweep balances may count (written policy required). Trading *into* a margin call is permitted [3][27]. Legacy PDT's 90-day cash-only freeze is replaced by the 90-day new-debit/new-short freeze; the $25,000 threshold and the 4-in-5 count are gone [3].
27. **Shorting inventory (CONTESTED).** The margin doc documents an HTB locate workflow: locate quotes, `POST /v1/locates` (round lots of 100, single-use, non-refundable, `limit_price`/`all_or_none`), 5,000+ ETB symbols with **$0 locate and borrow fees**, HTB daily borrow `Σ(short MV × HTB rate / 360)` charged in round lots with 3 days over weekends; locate endpoints are **not available in paper trading** [5][19]. But the **fee schedule revised 2026-09-01 lists "Hard to Borrow Securities — (Not currently available)"**, while paper trading shows borrow fees as "coming soon" [2][21]. The docs and the fee schedule disagree; assume ETB-only shorting in practice.

### 5. Market data plans and stream limits

28. **Plans (PROVEN).** Trading API has exactly two tiers: **Basic (free)** and **Algo Trader Plus ($99/mo)** [8][9]. Basic: IEX real-time only; 30 websocket symbols; historical since 2016; SIP historical blocked within the last 15 minutes; **200 historical API calls/min**. ATP: all US exchanges (CTA/UTP SIP) + OPRA for options; unlimited websocket symbols; no 15-minute restriction; **10,000 historical API calls/min** [8][9][16]. Options: Basic gets the indicative feed with 200 quote subscriptions; ATP gets OPRA with 1,000 [8]. Feeds selectable by `feed=`: `iex`, `sip`, `delayed_sip`, `boats` (Blue Ocean ATS, overnight), `overnight` (Alpaca's derived, 15-minute-delayed, spread-adjusted overnight feed), `otc` (broker-only) [15][33]. Latest endpoints *require* a subscription to use SIP; historical SIP needs `end` ≥ 15 minutes old; the default feed is always the best the subscription allows [16].
29. **Bars/limits (PROVEN).** Historical bars `limit` defaults to **1000**, max **10000**, applies to total data points across all symbols, paginate with `next_page_token`; results sort by symbol then timestamp, so one symbol can consume a whole page [34]. Bars are aggregated from trades using CTS/UTP condition rules; extended-hours trades (`T`,`U`) do update minute-bar OHLCV, and bars are emitted only if no OHLCV field is 0 — so a bar can be *missing* for a quiet minute [16]. Renames need `asof` (default enabled) and streaming clients must resubscribe to new symbols without reconnecting [16].
30. **Stream mechanics (PROVEN).** Market-data stream `wss://stream.data.alpaca.markets/{version}/{feed}`; control frames always length 1, data frames may be batched >1; `Content-Type: application/msgpack` and RFC-7692 compression supported; authenticate within **10 seconds** (as a message or via headers); test stream `.../v2/test` symbol `FAKEPACA` [14]. Channels: `trades`, `quotes`, `bars` (minute, emitted after each minute mark), `updatedBars` (after each half-minute if a late trade arrived), `dailyBars`, plus automatic `corrections`/`cancelErrors` when trades are subscribed [15]. **Connection limit**: most subscriptions allow **1** concurrent connection; a second yields `{"T":"error","code":406,"msg":"connection limit exceeded"}` [14].
31. **Stream error codes (PROVEN).** 400 invalid syntax, 401 not authenticated, 402 auth failed, 403 already authenticated, 404 auth timeout, 405 **symbol limit exceeded** (subscriptions revert to the previous set), 406 connection limit, 407 **slow client** (may be disconnected without warning), 409 insufficient subscription, 410 invalid channel for feed, 500 internal [14].
32. **Trade-updates stream (PROVEN/RECONSTRUCTED).** Trading account updates (fills, partial fills, cancels, rejects) arrive on `wss://paper-api.alpaca.markets/stream` / `wss://api.alpaca.markets/stream` after sending `{"action":"authenticate","data":{"key_id":...,"secret_key":...}}` and `{"action":"listen","data":{"streams":["trade_updates"]}}`; the ack is `{"stream":"listening",...}` [23]. Alpaca docs state that streaming is **the recommended method for maintaining order state** [1]. Note the auth envelope differs from the market-data stream (`auth`/`key`/`secret`), and Alpaca's current docs site no longer publishes a standalone trade-updates page (the historical `/docs/streaming-order-updates` URL 404s) — treat the SDK source and the 2026 OpenAPI description as the authoritative contract [1][13][23].
33. **Reliability (REPORTED).** Alpaca engineering traced a wave of `connection reset by peer` stream drops to an **ingress-server bug fixed ~12:00 ET on 2026-02-05**; the fix is not a guarantee against other causes, and the platform still relies on clients doing heartbeat + exponential-backoff reconnect + resubscribe because streams are live-only with no replay of the gap [14][17][23-ref: PR]. alpaca-py 0.44.0 shipped "Fix streaming reconnect reliability" (PR #744) [22].

## Contested or unproven

- **HTB shorting availability (CONTESTED).** The margin/short-selling guide documents a full locate workflow with HTB borrow math [5], while the Alpaca Brokerage Fee Schedule revised 2026-09-01 states "Hard to Borrow Securities — (Not currently available)" and paper lists borrow fees as "coming soon" [2][21]. Design as if only ETB (5,000+ symbols, $0 fees) is shortable, and treat any HTB expectation as unproven.
- **`client_order_id` reuse window (UNVERIFIED).** Alpaca's error text says uniqueness is enforced against "another active order" [17], but no doc states whether a string can be recycled after terminal state. Assume global uniqueness; do not use it as an idempotency key — Alpaca offers no `Idempotency-Key` on order endpoints [19].
- **OTO replacement (CONTESTED).** The OTO section says order replacement is "not supported yet" while the bracket/OCO sections say `PATCH` supports `limit_price`/`stop_price`, and the OTO paragraph immediately adds "Like bracket orders, order replacement is not supported yet" [1]. Verify empirically before depending on it.
- **Fractional + bracket/OCO (UNVERIFIED-but-probably-rejected).** No Alpaca page enumerates fractional advanced orders; the fractional page limits fractional support to `market|limit|stop|stop_limit` with `day` [4] and the error guide limits `notional` to those four types with `day` [17]. Treat fractional/notional bracket/OCO/OTO as unsupported until demonstrated.
- **`effective_buying_power` and `position_market_value` (UNVERIFIED).** Present in the documented `GET /v2/account` 200-example but absent from the schema properties, so no semantics are guaranteed [10][13]. Do not gate risk on them.
- **Trading API rate-limit number (REPORTED, stale).** 200 req/min comes from a December-2022 support article and the legacy SDK, plus the 2026 data-plan page for data calls [9][29]; no 2026 Trading API doc states the number, and Broker API deliberately publishes none [28]. Read the `X-RateLimit-*` headers rather than trusting 200.

## Implications for our system

1. **Make `multiplier`, not PDT flags, the capital contract.** The Phase-A gate must read `account.multiplier` (1/2/4), `account.buying_power`, `account.regt_buying_power`, `account.maintenance_margin`/`last_maintenance_margin`, `shorting_enabled`, `trading_blocked`, `status`, and refuse to load any code path that dereferences `pattern_day_trader` / `daytrading_buying_power` / `daytrade_count` — those keys stopped being returned on 2026-07-06 [18]. Add a startup assertion that logs `multiplier` so a 1x account cannot silently halve every sleeve.
2. **Cap each sleeve on the overnight (2x) number, not the intraday (4x) number.** `buying_power` with `multiplier=4` is `(last_equity − last_maintenance_margin) × 4` and DTBP does not apply to the overnight session [10][12]. The house risk gate should hand the Value-Dip sleeve a 2x-derived budget and the Intraday Algo sleeve a 4x-derived budget that decay to 2x by 20:00 ET, so an unfilled overnight day order cannot trigger an IMD.
3. **Encode the order lattice as a validator, not as documented convention.** Implement a single `OrderRequest` factory that rejects at construction: stop/stop-limit with ioc/fok/opg/cls; `extended_hours=true` with anything but `limit`+`day|gtc`; bracket/oco/oto with `extended_hours`, `notional`, or non-`day|gtc` TIF; fractional `qty` with `gtc`; any fractional `sell` intended to open a short; `qty`+`notional` together; notional < $1.00. alpaca-py 0.44.0 now fails *client-side* on the price-field subset — rely on that but do not assume it covers the session/TIF/class intersections [1][4][22][30].
4. **Price rounding belongs in the sizing layer.** Round limit/stop prices to 2 decimals at ≥ $1.00 and 4 decimals below, and place stop-loss legs ≥ $0.01 beyond the base price, or the API returns 42210000 and the sleeve silently loses an exit [1]. A sub-penny-tick error on a stop is a naked position, not a validation nuisance.
5. **Give each sleeve its own `client_order_id` namespace and a query-before-retry reconciler.** `{sleeve}-{strategy}-{signal_id}-{attempt}` must be unique forever (duplicate → 422) and doubles as the ledger key for `GET /v2/orders:by_client_order_id`. On 5xx/timeout, the daemon must **query state first** (official guidance) and never blind-resend; replace is non-atomic (success ≠ replaced) and unusable for notional orders, so exits must be cancel-then-resubmit [1][13][17][26].
6. **Treat the wash-trade guard as a first-class intraday failure mode.** Two of our own orders on the same symbol that could interact return 403, except bracket/OCO/trailing-stop [11]. The Intraday Algo sleeve must therefore express "take profit + stop" as a bracket or OCO at entry time (or a trailing stop) rather than posting an independent contra limit; a `value-dip` resting order in the other sleeve becomes an unbudgeted wash-trade risk if both sleeves trade the same ticker — add a cross-sleeve symbol lock in the house gate.
7. **Model IML/IMD as the second house gate beside CVaR.** Read `equity`, `maintenance_margin`, `intraday_adjustments` and `last_equity` before every order batch, compute IML excess, and refuse batches whose projected deficit exceeds the de-minimis band (< $1,000 or < 5% of equity) — that band is the practical intraday kill-switch, and an IMD gives us days (2 business days per Alpaca; capital charge from day 6) rather than hours [3][27].
8. **Add the real cost stack to the sleeve comparison harness now.** The 2026-01-02 → 2026-09-11 comparison must subtract SEC $20.60/$1M (sells), TAF $0.000195/share capped $9.79 (sells), CAT $0.000003/share (both sides), ADR pass-throughs, and short carry (ETB $0; assume HTB unavailable), then re-derive Sharpe/Sortino on the net series. Without this the Intraday Algo sleeve — which turns over far more and is short-biased — is systematically flattered relative to Value-Dip, exactly the apples-to-oranges error the brief exists to prevent [2][21].
9. **Data tier decision is a hard dependency, not a tuning knob.** Basic cannot see consolidated tape and cannot subscribe beyond 30 symbols on 1 connection; the last 15 minutes of SIP history are blocked [8][14][16]. For a genuine intraday sleeve on a universe >30 names, budget the $99/mo ATP tier explicitly in the sleeve P&L, and if we stay on IEX-only, restrict the Intraday Algo universe to symbols where IEX prints dominate or the comparison is measuring IEX noise, not strategy.
10. **Harden the daemon's stream layer before wiring orders.** Run one market-data connection per account (406 on the second), authenticate within 10 s, treat 405/407 as subscription/throughput failures, and add the watchdog alpaca-py lacks on `trade_updates` (no `data_timeout`) — reconnect with exponential backoff + resubscribe, then reconcile order state by REST because streams have no replay across gaps and Alpaca had an ingress bug as recently as 2026-02-05 [14][17][23].
11. **Scope any MCP/agent surface to toolsets that cannot trade.** `ALPACA_TOOLSETS` is the only allow-list and there is no read-only flag; `account` also contains `update_account_config`, so a genuinely read-only agent surface must omit `account` and `trading` and keep `ALPACA_PAPER_TRADE=true` [6][7]. No order-submission logic should ever be reachable through MCP; the LLM layers stay on the research side of the API contract.
